How the score works. Each person's score for a day is 1 − e−W/5, where W is the summed weight of every artist on that bill they actually listen to. The pair's score is the minimum of the two, not the average — so one person's zero caps the day no matter how much the other loves it. Weights come from Spotify top-artist rank plus how often an artist appears across saved tracks and playlists.
